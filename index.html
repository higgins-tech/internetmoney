What is The Internet Money Ecosystem?

The center of our ecosystem is the Internet Money Wallet. A multi-chain EVM wallet, native to PulseChain. Privacy, Security and Simplicity focused, with PASSIVE INCOME opportunities through our innovative tokens.

Internet Money Wallet

The Internet Money Wallet does not collect ANY user data. No KYC required. Your wallet is your gateway to DeFi - send/receive/swap your favorite crypto assets. Connect to ALL EVM chains (with many pre-loaded). Track the price of ALL EVM tokens/coins. Connect to your favorite dApps and earn passive income by holding TIME!

T.I.M.E. Dividend (TIME)

Holders of TIME earn passive income in PLS, ETH, BNB, MATIC, and AVAX. 100% of the fees generated from in-app swaps are distributed to TIME holders. No staking. No locking your TIME. Simply hold your TIME, and earn passive income. Available on Pulsechain, Ethereum, Base, BSC, Polygon, Avalanche, and Arbitrum.

Internet Money (IM)

The Internet Money (IM) token is deflationary, peer-to-peer, digital cash. It's money, on the internet. Deflationary due to a buy and burn mechanic based on wallet swap volume! Completely decentralized. Available on Pulsechain, BSC and Ethereum.

Future T.I.M.E. Dividend (FUTURE)

As TIME is launched on new EVM chains, 100% of the supply will be airdropped to FUTURE holders at a rate of 1:1 based on their FUTURE holdings. FUTURE is available on Ethereum and Pulsechain.

The Internet Money Wallet

The Internet Money Wallet is the first crypto wallet of its kind. Dedicated to providing users with the cleanest and most approachable user experience, the Internet Money Wallet offers features that crypto users of all experience levels will appreciate.

As a multi-chain EVM wallet, the Internet Money Wallet supports all Ethereum-based blockchains so that you can invest in, hold, swap, send, receive and track the prices of the tokens you want, all in one place; and earn passive income at the same time.
